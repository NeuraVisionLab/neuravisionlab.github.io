Many possible futures. One shared scene.

Motion depends on more than where someone has been. People and vehicles respond to one another and to the space around them, so the same observed trajectory can lead to several plausible futures.

Our research studies human trajectory forecasting and autonomous-driving scenarios, with a focus on predicting multiple possible outcomes and modeling interactions between agents. We aim to capture future motion that is plausible for each agent and consistent with the surrounding scene.
